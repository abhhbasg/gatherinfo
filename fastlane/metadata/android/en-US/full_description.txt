The Windows Phone keyboard, on Android, made to work together with MetroWin Launcher.

• Flat Metro keys with big light type, in three looks: Classic, Modern Glass and Modern Droid, with the 20 Windows Phone colours or your own, or a photo behind the keys
• Swipe typing, hold a key for accents and symbols, double space for a full stop, slide on the space bar to move the cursor
• Correction that you can undo with Backspace, next-word and phrase prediction, offensive words blocked, everything learned on your phone only
• 18 languages included, Simplified Chinese (Pinyin and Microsoft Shuangpin, with whole-sentence input) among them; add any language or dictionary yourself
• Translate what you wrote, or what you just copied, between languages (Google by default; Google Cloud, DeepL, LibreTranslate or MyMemory if you prefer)
• Clipboard history with pictures, a quick button on the bar, emoji and classic emoticons, GIFs from the source you choose, voice typing
• Adjustable key height, width and spacing; sound (Windows Phone style) and vibration with adjustable strength
• A guided set-up and a tutorial, in the app and inside the keyboard

No ads, no analytics, no trackers. Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). Not affiliated with Microsoft.
